Black/White inspired rules & limitations

A level playing field

Generation V types and mechanics, with historical Black/White learnsets and levels 1–100. Each contender uses 31 IVs, zero EVs, a neutral nature and base PP. Battles have one Pokémon per side, with no held items, abilities, reserve team or multiplayer.

Know your moves

Choose up to four legal moves, including Black/White machine and breeding moves. Move effects include weather, multi-hit and charging attacks, protection, Substitute, Transform and status conditions. Moves needing a held item, reserve teammate or another missing condition can fail, just as in the games. An empty moveset or exhausted PP triggers Struggle.

Play, learn, replay

Generation V battle mechanics run locally using Pokémon Showdown. Battles draw after 200 turns. Simulations never change caught records or caught-based ranks. Only qualified, server-prepared recordings count toward separate activity achievements.
